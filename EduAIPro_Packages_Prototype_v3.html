Good to know

Plans last 2 years

You pay once and your plan covers 24 months from the day you pay. Every certificate you earn is valid until your plan's end date.

6 months per pathway

Finish each pathway within 6 months of opening its first slide: about 30 hours, at your own pace. On a paid plan you get one automatic 6-month extension. On Free, your progress is saved and you can upgrade to continue.

Your certificate is yours

If your plan ends or you change schools, every certificate you've earned stays valid until its expiry date.

We'll keep you on track

Progress reminders while you learn, a countdown before your 6 months are up, and a reminder before your plan and certificate end.

Refreshers need an active plan

A refresher is a short course that renews your certificate when it is due. Keep your plan active, or renew it, before you start one.

6-month rule

  • Clock starts when the first slide of a pathway is opened.
  • Reminders: month 1, 3 and 5 if behind pace; then 30, 7 and 1 day before the 6-month mark. Dashboard shows "Finish by [date] to be certified until [plan end]".
  • Free at 6 months: pathway "Expired", progress kept, content and quizzes closed. Upgrading reopens it with a new 6 months.
  • Paid at 6 months: one automatic 6-month extension, weekly nudges. At 12 months (or at plan end, if sooner): pathway locks, progress kept; reopening needs a renewal or support.
  • Finished but unpaid: certificate claimable for 6 months after finishing, then final assessments must be retaken.
  • Certificate expiry = plan end date. Before starting a pathway with under 6 months of plan left, warn: "Your certificate would be valid for about N months. Renew now to add 24 months."

Free school demo

  • A demo is a school account with plan = demo, not a separate account type.
  • Limits: 5 invites, Unit 1 only, 30 days, "Demo" label on the dashboard. Full tracking for invited teachers.
  • Teacher tries to open Unit 2: paywall, "Your school is trialling EduAI Pro. Ask your school to activate your seat, or get Personal Pro." The school admin gets a notification at the same time.
  • Buying seats upgrades the same account; demo teachers keep progress and get seats first.
  • At 30 days: dashboard read-only, pending invites cancelled; teachers stay as Free teachers.
  • One demo per school (email domain + phone). Internal admin can list, extend and convert demos.
